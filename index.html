The number that matters is the last column
Divided three ways, the largest cheque a buyer has to write falls from $467,000 to $168,000. In Tbilisi that is the difference between the thinnest segment of the market and the deepest one — and it is why three apartments sell in weeks where one takes months.
  • Sell in stages, or not at allRelease one apartment to recover capital and keep the other two. One asset cannot do that.
  • Three tenancies, not oneA vacancy costs a third of the income rather than all of it, and small apartments let faster to a far larger pool.
  • Be clear on what it is notTotal value sits within 3.5% across all three configurations, and the extra fit-out slightly reduces the yield. Division buys liquidity and optionality — not a profit on paper.
This is a green-frame decision, and only a green-frame decision
Nothing is tiled, floored or fitted yet. Moving a partition and adding a door now costs what the partition and the door cost. The same change after a $87,500 fit-out means demolishing finished rooms. The option exists today and closes the day the finishing starts.
